How will you check your layout?

Measure your input beam. Design a layout, build it, then check the measured beam radius against your prediction.

If the difference is larger than the scatter in your measurements, measure the input again. If they agree, keep the layout link and record why you chose it.

What can you learn from one image?

You can measure the beam width at the camera. To find where the beam is narrowest, move the camera and measure at several positions.

Glossary

²⁵Mg⁺
A magnesium-25 atom with one electron removed. The ultraviolet light is for these ions.
Doubling cavity
Mirrors circulate light through a crystal. The crystal converts some of the light to twice its frequency, or half its wavelength.
Spatial overlap
The fraction of incident power that matches the shape and wavefront of the light the cavity supports, assuming perfect alignment.
Gaussian beam
A beam whose intensity falls away from its centre in a bell shape. Its narrowest cross section is called the waist.
